About me

I’m Chirag Salvi, a professional martial arts coach and self-defense educator with over 12 years of experience training individuals, corporates and institutions in real-world safety, combat readiness and performance conditioning.

As Founder of Battle Fit & Battle Fit Academy, I’ve conducted 600+ self-defense workshops and awareness programs across the corporate, educational and NGO sectors — empowering 1,000+ professionals, students and athletes.

My teaching blends Judo, Krav Maga, Kickboxing, Kalaripayattu and Functional Fitness, focused on awareness, adaptability and control.
